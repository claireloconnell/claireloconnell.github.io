One of the unexpected joys of studying such a highly social species and focusing on something as recognizable as relationship formation is how easily people relate to the topic. After our [paper](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsbl/article/21/11/20250399/234062/Monk-parakeets-test-the-waters-when-forming-new) was published, the work resonated widely, and several news outlets picked it up and shared their own perspectives on our findings.
